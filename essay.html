My Favorite Crickter: Babar Azam

Childhood & Early Life:

Babar Azam was born in Lahore in 1994, and from his childhood he showed an exceptional passion for cricket. He grew up near the famous Gaddafi Stadium, where he watched players like Inzamam-ul-Haq and Younis Khan. His strong dedication pushed him to practice for hours every day, often under the scorching sun. At the age of 15 th, he joined a local cricket academy where his coach recognized his emphasize discipline and talent.He played under-15 and under-19 tournaments, showing his potential early on. Today, his journey from street cricket to the Pakistan national team inspires millions.

Achievements & Records:

Babar Azam made his international debut in 2015, and since then he has achieved remarkable success. He became the fastest batter to reach 5,000 ODI runs, surpassing Virat Kohli and Hashim Amla. His batting average in ODIs is one of the strongest in modern cricket, and he is known for his stylish cover drives. He has led Pakistan to memorable victories, including winning the ICC Champions Trophy. Here's a short list of his top achievements:

  1. Fastest to 1000 T20 run
  2. ICC Men's Player of the Year 2022
  3. Consistent top-ranked ODI
